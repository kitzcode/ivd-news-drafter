## 1. Authorization of Emergency Use of an In Vitro Diagnostic Device in Response to an Outbreak of Mpox; Availability

**Why:** FDA EUA issuance for mpox IVD—direct regulatory action on diagnostic device authorization

**Summary:** FDA issued an Emergency Use Authorization for the Allplex HSV-1&2/VZV/MPXV Assay, which can simultaneously detect and differentiate MPXV (clade I and II), HSV-1, HSV-2, and VZV from a single test. This builds on the HHS public health emergency determination made in August 2022 related to monkeypox. The assay is now authorized for emergency diagnostic use under the FD&C Act.

**Draft:**

FDA just issued an EUA for the Allplex HSV-1&2/VZV/MPXV Assay, which does simultaneous detection and differentiation of MPXV (clade I and II), HSV-1, HSV-2, and VZV in one run.

The part that caught my eye as someone who works in diagnostics: that combination. Lesion presentations for these can look a lot alike clinically, so being able to rule in or out multiple targets at once matters for how quickly a clinician can act.

Still reading through the authorization conditions (those details are usually where the interesting constraints live).
