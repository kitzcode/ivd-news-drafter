## 2. It Takes More Than Insurance Coverage to Standardize NGS Testing in Practice, Study Shows

**Why:** NGS testing standardization and clinical implementation challenges relevant to lab regulation

**Summary:** A study reported by 360Dx found that insurance coverage alone is not enough to standardize NGS testing in clinical practice. Other barriers beyond reimbursement exist that affect whether and how NGS testing gets used consistently.

**Draft:**

There's a study out (via 360Dx) about NGS testing standardization, and the headline is basically: insurance coverage is necessary but not sufficient.

That tracks with what I see on the data side. Coverage decisions show up cleanly in claims. What doesn't show up as cleanly is everything else: whether the test was ordered, interpreted consistently, acted on. Reimbursement is legible. The rest is messier.

Not a surprising finding, honestly. But useful to see it documented rather than just assumed.

**Source:** 360Dx — https://www.360dx.com/cancer/it-takes-more-insurance-coverage-standardize-ngs-testing-practice-study-shows
