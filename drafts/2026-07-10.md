**Summary:** The FDA granted Breakthrough Device designation to Zepto Life Technologies for a blood test designed to detect invasive mold infections. The designation is intended to speed development and review of diagnostics that address serious conditions with unmet needs.

**Draft:**

Zepto Life Technologies just got FDA Breakthrough Device designation for a blood test for invasive mold infections. That's a pretty specific niche, but invasive mold (think Aspergillus in immunocompromised patients) is genuinely hard to diagnose fast enough to matter, so the unmet need is real. Blood-based, earlier signal, less invasive than tissue sampling. I'll be watching to see what the performance data looks like when it gets further along. Breakthrough designation doesn't mean it's there yet, just that FDA thinks it's worth moving faster on.
