# IVD / diagnostics drafts — 2026-11-09

## 1. Recommendations for Reducing the Risk of Transfusion-Transmitted Hepatitis B Virus; Guidance for Industry; Availability

**Why:** FDA guidance on hepatitis B virus testing for blood products—directly relevant to IVD testing requirements and blood bank regulations.

**Summary:** FDA has finalized guidance on reducing transfusion-transmitted hepatitis B virus risk. It consolidates and supersedes multiple older documents going back to 1987, covering testing recommendations for whole blood, blood components, and source plasma. The final guidance updates a draft from July 2025.

**Draft:**

FDA just finalized guidance on reducing transfusion-transmitted hepatitis B virus risk in blood and blood components (including Source Plasma).

What catches my eye: this one document supersedes guidance and memos going all the way back to 1987. Some of those have been sitting as the operative reference for blood establishments for a long time.

Consolidation like this is genuinely useful from a practical standpoint. Fewer documents to track, clearer current expectations. (Though I always wonder how long it takes the SOPs and training materials downstream to actually catch up.)
