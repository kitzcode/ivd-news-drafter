# IVD / diagnostics drafts — 2026-11-23

## 1. Product-Specific Guidances; Draft and Revised Draft Guidances for Industry; Availability  (low confidence)

**Why:** 

**Summary:** FDA announced the availability of new draft and revised draft product-specific guidances for industry. These guidances provide recommendations on designing bioequivalence studies to support abbreviated new drug applications (ANDAs). The process follows the framework FDA established back in 2010 for making product-specific guidances publicly available.

**Draft:**

FDA dropped another batch of draft product-specific guidances this week, the ones that spell out how bioequivalence studies should be designed for specific products when you're filing an ANDA.

Not the flashiest regulatory update, but if you work in generics development or CMC, these matter. The recommendations are pretty granular (study design, endpoints, that kind of thing), and getting them wrong early is expensive.

I mainly flagged it because it's easy to miss a new batch in the Federal Register noise. Worth a scan if any of your products are in scope.

**Source:** Federal Register — https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23692/product-specific-guidances-draft-and-revised-draft-guidances-for-industry-availability

## 2. Revocation of the Color Additive Listing for Use of Orange B on Casings or Surfaces of Frankfurters and Sausages; Confirmation of Effective Date  (low confidence)

**Why:** 

**Summary:** FDA has confirmed the effective date of September 8, 2026, for a rule revoking the approval of Orange B, a color additive that had been permitted for use on the casings or surfaces of frankfurters and sausages. The final order was published in the Federal Register on July 23, 2026.

**Draft:**

FDA confirmed the revocation of Orange B as a permitted color additive for frankfurter and sausage casings, effective September 8, 2026.

I'll be honest, I had no idea Orange B was a thing that existed and was used on hot dog casings. Apparently it was, and now it isn't.

Not a dramatic story, just a quiet regulatory housekeeping item that I noticed while keeping tabs on food additive listings. (The kind of thing you start tracking when your work touches product labeling and ingredient data, and then you can't stop.)
