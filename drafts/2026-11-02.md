# IVD / diagnostics drafts — 2026-11-02

## 1. Medical Devices; Radiology Devices; Classification of the Radiology Software for Referral of Findings Related to Fibrotic Lung Disease

**Why:** FDA classification order for radiology software device; directly relevant to diagnostic device regulation and FDA 510(k) pathway

**Summary:** FDA has classified AI/software tools that flag findings related to fibrotic lung disease on radiology images as Class II medical devices (special controls). The agency says this classification provides reasonable assurance of safety and effectiveness while reducing regulatory burden and improving patient access.

**Draft:**

FDA just classified radiology software that flags fibrotic lung disease findings as Class II (special controls). Not cleared, not de novo — classified, which means there's now a defined regulatory home for this device type with specific controls attached.

Fibrotic lung disease is notoriously tricky to catch early, and the read burden on radiologists is real. So software that helps surface those findings has obvious appeal.

I work adjacent to this space (diagnostics, clinical data, software), and mostly what I notice is that "reduces regulatory burden" doing a lot of work in that Federal Register language. We'll see what the special controls actually require.

**Source:** Federal Register — https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/30/2026-22180/medical-devices-radiology-devices-classification-of-the-radiology-software-for-referral-of-findings
