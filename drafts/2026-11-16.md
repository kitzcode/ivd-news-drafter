# IVD / diagnostics drafts — 2026-11-16

## 1. Validation of Certain In Vitro Diagnostic Devices for Emerging Pathogens During a Section 564 Declared Emergency; Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff; Availability

**Why:** FDA guidance on IVD validation for emerging pathogens under emergency use authorizations—directly relevant to diagnostic device regulation and emergency response protocols.

**Summary:** FDA has finalized a guidance document on how IVD manufacturers should validate tests for emerging pathogens during a Section 564 public health emergency declaration. It covers validation recommendations for pre-EUA submissions, EUA requests, and tests offered under enforcement discretion policies. A template accompanies the guidance.

**Draft:**

FDA just finalized guidance on IVD validation for emerging pathogens during a Section 564 declared emergency. It covers what test data and information should look like for pre-EUA submissions, EUA requests, and enforcement discretion scenarios, and comes with a template.

Not exactly beach reading, but if you work in diagnostics and ever had to figure out validation requirements mid-emergency (while everything else was also on fire), having this written down in one place is genuinely useful.

I'll be reading the template first, honestly.
